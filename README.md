**WATER BILLING SYSTEM**



**Introduction**
A Water Billing System is a computer-based system used by water supply companies to calculate charges based on customers’ water consumption. This system helps to replace the manual system with a digital system in order to improve efficiency and reduce errors.



**Problem Statement**
In the current manual system, errors often occur during the calculation of water bills. In addition, the process of generating bills is time-consuming, and customer records may sometimes be lost or mixed up. This creates inconvenience for both customers and staff.
